The headline scenario runs Microsoft's official applications/storm_impact_assessment/hurricane_forecast_infra_impact.ipynb, pre-wired to the Azure resources this solution deploys. Aurora predicts the track; StormLens shows which assets are at risk.

Seven steps

How a forecast is produced

Storm selection

Pick any historical storm from IBTrACS (Hurricane Helene 2024 is pre-selected) or hook an active feed.

ECMWF data download

Retrieve HRES weather data through the Planetary Computer Pro STAC API.

Aurora batch preparation

Format the atmospheric variables into the Aurora model's input batch.

Aurora inference

Run hurricane-track prediction on the Aurora model hosted on an Azure AI Foundry GPU endpoint (A100).

Track visualization

Compare predicted vs. observed paths; write model outputs to your model-outputs storage container with managed identity.

Infrastructure analysis

Intersect the storm swath with power / energy assets from OpenStreetMap to rank what is in the path.

Publish & explore

Ingest results into the GeoCatalog and view them on the branded StormLens map.

🌐

Data sources

IBTrACS / HURDAT storm tracks · ECMWF HRES weather (via MPC) · OpenStreetMap power infrastructure.

📦

Model outputs

Forecast artifacts are written to your storage account's model-outputs container — keyless, via the workstation's managed identity.

🛢️

Oil & gas relevance

Rank offshore platforms, refineries, pipelines, and grid assets by storm exposure for evacuation and shut-in decisions.

Prerequisite for live forecasts: the Aurora model on a Foundry GPU endpoint (Standard_NC24ads_A100_v4) with quota + accepted marketplace terms. The rest of the solution — catalog, workstation, storage, this site — runs without it, and the Live Explorer works against any collection in your catalog.
