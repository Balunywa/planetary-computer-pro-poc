StormLens is a turnkey weather-intelligence solution for energy and oil & gas operators. It predicts a tropical storm's path with the Aurora AI weather model, pulls open weather & imagery through Microsoft Planetary Computer Pro, and maps the impact on offshore platforms, refineries, pipelines, and the power grid — all in your own Azure tenant, governed by your identity.

Why this stack

Open geospatial data + a foundation weather model, in your tenant

No more emailing GRIB files or screenshotting a public portal. One governed catalog, one identity model, from raw feed to the operator map.

🗄️

One catalog

Planetary Computer Pro (GeoCatalog) stores, indexes, and serves imagery & weather via the open STAC standard.

🌀

Aurora AI

Microsoft's foundation weather model runs hurricane-track inference on an Azure AI Foundry GPU endpoint.

⚡

Infrastructure impact

Storm tracks are intersected with energy / power assets (OpenStreetMap) to rank what's in the path.

🔒

Governed by default

Microsoft Entra ID + managed identity everywhere — no account keys, no public RDP, private Bastion access.
